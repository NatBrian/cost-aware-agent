   VERDICT: REGULARISER. The treatment answers single-hop fact
   lookups better, where there is no multi-hop reasoning to improve.
   The quality gain is GENERIC and must be reported as a confound,
   not as a benefit of cost-aware training.
   The STOPPING result is unaffected -- T1 showed Δsteps and ΔF1 are
   independent -- but the paper may claim only the stopping effect.

4. note on Δsteps, which this test does NOT hinge on
   The agent spends >3 steps on single-hop questions and leaves most
   unanswered, so doomed work exists here too and a negative Δsteps is
   consistent with cost-awareness rather than evidence against it.
